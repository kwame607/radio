Frequently Asked Questions

How can I listen to Angel 96.1FM live?

Head to our Listen Live & Contact page. You can watch/listen live via our official Facebook page, or stream our sister station Angel 102.9FM Accra directly from the page while our own dedicated audio stream is being set up.

Where is Angel 96.1FM based?

We broadcast from Abrepo-Junction, Kumasi, Ghana.

What are your operating hours?

We're on air 24 hours a day, every day — see our full Program Schedule for what's playing when.

How do I contact the station?

Call or WhatsApp us at +233 24 667 7566, email wod351@gmail.com, or use the contact form on our Listen Live & Contact page.

How can I advertise on Angel 96.1FM?

We'd love to hear from you — reach out via our contact form or WhatsApp and our team will get back to you about advertising slots and rates.

Can I request a song or shout-out?

Yes — reach out to us on WhatsApp or Facebook during the relevant show, and our presenters will do their best to include it on air.

I found an error on the website — who do I tell?

Please let us know via the contact form or WhatsApp so we can fix it quickly.
