Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Angel 96.1FM ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Contact form: when you send us a message via the Listen Live & Contact page, we collect your name, email address, phone number, subject, and message content.

Newsletter: if you subscribe, we collect your email address.

Automatic data: like most websites, our server and analytics tools (if enabled) may log standard technical information such as your browser type, device, and pages visited.

Local storage: we use your browser's local storage only to remember your dark/light mode preference. This stays on your device and is never sent to us.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, send our newsletter (if you've subscribed), and improve our website and programming. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party embeds

Our Listen Live page embeds content from Facebook (Meta Platforms, Inc.). When that content loads, Facebook may collect data according to its own privacy policy, independent of this one. We encourage you to review Facebook's privacy policy if you have concerns.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, by contacting us using the details below.
